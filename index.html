How it works

Companies report what they produced once a quarter or half-year. Plants and grids report it every day. We build each company from its plants up.

  1. 1

    Every plant

    Each company’s farms and solar parks, from its own reports, checked against the official registers.

  2. 2

    Its owner

    Who holds each plant, and at what share. Versioned, so an estimate uses the fleet as it stood that day.

  3. 3

    What it produced

    The plant’s own meter where one is published: 43 British wind farms every half hour, Danish farms every month. Elsewhere, the country’s output that day.

  4. 4

    The company

    Every plant times its share, summed, sealed before the company reports, and scored against its figure.

The method in full, with the receipts, is on the data page.

What arrives each morning

One vintage a day, built so it can be backtested honestly.

Granularity
Each company, by bidding zone and production type, per day
Point in time
Every row carries the day produced and the moment it was known. Rows are added, never changed
History
From 2021 or 2023, rebuilt with the same code; live since 13 November 2026
Format
Parquet files every morning, with a manifest of sources and their licence terms

Field definitions, how the history is built and every source are on the data page.
